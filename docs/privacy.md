Effective date: July 25, 2026

MD Viewer: Reading Window does not collect, store, sell, or share personal
data. It does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or an
external cloud service.

Markdown files are opened only after you select them in macOS or ask Finder to
open them with MD Viewer. Their content is processed locally on your Mac.
Mermaid diagrams are rendered with a copy of Mermaid bundled inside the app;
the app does not use a CDN. Printing and PDF creation use the standard macOS
printing system.

The app does not transmit document content or usage information to the
developer. The open-source project is hosted on GitHub; visiting GitHub or this
website is subject to the privacy terms of those services.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the
[MDPreview issue tracker](https://github.com/hshimomura/MDPreview/issues).
